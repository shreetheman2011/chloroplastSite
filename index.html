Energy policy

The light reactions

Every strong campaign needs funding, and Chloroplast raises capital the honest way: sunlight. Inside the thylakoid membrane, photosystems II and I grab incoming photons and put them straight to work, splitting water molecules apart and releasing oxygen as a public good along the way. The energy gets banked as ATP and NADPH, the two donors that bankroll everything the campaign does next.

Where it happens
Thylakoid membrane
What goes in
Sunlight, water
What comes out
ATP, NADPH, oxygen
Growth policy

The Calvin cycle

Once the funding lands, Chloroplast delivers on the promise of growth. In the stroma, an enzyme called rubisco pulls carbon dioxide straight out of the air and, powered by the ATP and NADPH from the light reactions, builds it into a molecule called G3P, the raw material for glucose, starch, and every leaf, root, and fruit downstream. It's carbon capture that actually works, running quietly in every leaf on Earth, no ribbon-cutting required.

Where it happens
Stroma
What goes in
CO₂, ATP, NADPH
What comes out
G3P → glucose & starch
Why it matters

Chloroplast's case for re-election

This isn't only about one cell's job security. Chloroplast's photosynthesis platform is the reason there's breathable air, the foundation of nearly every food chain on the planet, and a load-bearing pillar of the entire carbon cycle. Pull chloroplasts out of the picture and there's no oxygen surplus, no plants, no herbivores, and, a few links down the chain, no us.

Oxygen production

Photosynthesis is the source of the oxygen that fills the atmosphere, released as a byproduct of splitting water during the light reactions.

Base of the food chain

Chloroplasts let plants and algae make their own food from sunlight, making them the primary producers nearly every other organism eats, directly or indirectly.

Carbon cycle regulation

By pulling carbon dioxide out of the air during the Calvin cycle, chloroplasts help keep the planet's carbon budget in balance.

Cell survival

The sugar chloroplasts manufacture is the energy supply the rest of the plant cell runs on. No fuel, no functioning cell.
